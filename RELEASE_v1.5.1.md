# CyberGrip GLITCH v1.5.1 — UI FIX

First public GitHub release of CyberGrip GLITCH Camera for Android.

## Highlights
- Live glitch-camera effects and stackable FX
- Analog film looks, Film Lab and P1/P2/P3 presets
- Photo capture and MP4 video recording
- 1080p / 4K UHD output-size selection and 12 / 20 / 30 Mbps H.264 bitrate options
- Improved landscape capture button sizing (v1.5.1)

## Important limitations
- 1080p and 4K files are upscaled from a 960×720 processing source; **not native 4K**
- Frame rate can vary and recording may stutter, especially at 4K
- Video does not include audio
- Experimental build; test on your device

## Installation
Download the **signed APK** attached below and open it on a compatible Android device. You may need to allow installation from the app used to open the APK.
